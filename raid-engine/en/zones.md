# Zones

Zones are server-defined areas with special rules. Some servers use them to create safe regions, war arenas, or spawn areas.

## For players
- Use your server’s commands (if any) to check the current zone.
- Zones may show a greeting when you enter; follow the local rules.

## For administrators
- Zones are optional and implementation-specific. If you enable the clan “zone” upgrade, players can create small public areas inside their claim.
- For global zones (spawn, events), use your preferred region plugin and set messages/rules there. RaidEngine respects territory when applicable.
