# Portals & Warps

## For players
- Use `/f warp <name>` to teleport to your clan warps.
- Some servers auto-teleport when entering portal frames managed by a portals plugin.

## For administrators
- Clan warps are player-managed: `/f setwarp <name> <password>` and `/f delwarp <name>`.
- Global server portals are handled by your portals plugin; RaidEngine doesn’t create/manage them.
